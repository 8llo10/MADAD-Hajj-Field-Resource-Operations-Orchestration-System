# Operations Notes

MADAD coordinates incident response through dispatch, team readiness, and resource visibility.

## Operational flow

1. An incident is registered with site, zone, urgency, and required specialization.
2. The dispatch engine ranks available teams.
3. A dispatcher can use the proposed team or make a manual assignment.
4. Team and incident state changes are reflected in the operations center.
5. Resource movements and major actions are recorded for traceability.

## Dispatch factors

- Distance to the incident
- Specialization match
- Current workload
- Team readiness
- Vehicle availability
- Shift status
- SLA and urgency
