# MADAD Architecture

MADAD is organized around operational workflows for Hajj field response.

## Core modules

- Incident Management
- Dispatch Engine
- Team Management
- Sites and Zones
- Resources and Inventory
- Operations Center Dashboard
- Audit Logs and Reporting

## Dispatch ranking

Team ranking considers operational factors such as distance, specialization match, workload, readiness, vehicle availability, shift status, and incident urgency.

## Technology

- Backend: Express, Prisma, PostgreSQL
- Frontend: Next.js
- Realtime: WebSockets
- Deployment: Render and Vercel
