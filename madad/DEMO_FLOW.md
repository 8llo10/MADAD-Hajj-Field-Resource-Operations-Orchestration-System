# Demo Flow for Kadana

1. Login as `admin@madad.sa / Madad@123`.
2. Open **البلاغات** and point to the critical electrical outage in Mina.
3. Open **التوجيه الذكي** and select `INC-2026-001`.
4. Show ranked teams: each team has score /100, distance, ETA, breakdown and reasons.
5. Explain that the recommended team is not chosen by distance alone; availability, specialization, required skills, workload and exact site/zone are considered together.
6. Click **اقتراح أفضل فرقة تلقائيًا** to persist the recommendation.
7. Show resources and inventory readiness.
8. Return to Command Center and explain realtime operational picture.
9. Show Reports for SLA and response indicators.

Suggested one-line pitch:
> مَدَد يحول البلاغ من معلومة منفصلة إلى قرار تشغيلي: من الأقرب والأقدر والمتاح الآن، وما الموارد التي يحتاجها، وكيف نتابع التنفيذ حتى الإغلاق.
